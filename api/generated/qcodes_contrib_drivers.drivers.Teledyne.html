class qcodes_contrib_drivers.drivers.Teledyne.T3PS3000.TeledyneT3PS3000(*args: Any, **kwargs: Any)[source]¶

Bases: VisaInstrument

Teledyne LeCroy T3PS3000 three-channel DC power supply.

CH1 and CH2 support independent, series, and parallel operation. CH3 has front-panel voltage selection and write-only remote output control. Construction and closing the connection do not change output settings. Writes wait 100 ms after transmission before returning so subsequent commands do not arrive too soon. Queries wait one second before reading, including the initial identification query, to allow the supply time to prepare its response.

Parameters:

  • name – Instrument name.

  • address – VISA resource address (USB or LAN).

Teledyne LeCroy T3PS30051P single-channel, 30 V / 5 A DC power supply.

Construction does not reset the supply or change its output. Closing the connection does not turn the output off. Identification uses the known vendor and model without sending the unsupported *IDN? query; serial number and firmware are unavailable. Writes wait 100 ms after transmission before returning so subsequent commands do not arrive too soon. Queries wait one second before reading the response.

Parameters:

  • name – Instrument name.

  • address – VISA resource address (USB or LAN).

  • **kwargs – Forwarded to qcodes.instrument.VisaInstrument.
